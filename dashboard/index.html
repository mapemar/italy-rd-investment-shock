How to interpret these figures

  • €194.4bn is the overall PNRR envelope, not R&D spending.
  • €8.55bn is the MUR M4C2 allocation, not realised GERD.
  • €180.2m is reported economies for six MUR measures; it is not a negative R&D shock.
  • €7.102bn is a Treasury return profile, not an expenditure reduction in the return years.
  • The €1.588bn ministry-level annex does not identify individual measures.
Critical rule: allocations, appropriations, commitments, cash payments, beneficiary expenditure and R&D performed are separate stages.
