# M4C2 aggregate R&D quantification — preliminary v0.1

**Run date:** 20 November 2026  
**Status:** preliminary model output; not validated as an official R&D expenditure series  
**Scope:** M4C2 research-oriented investments, plus separately identified PRIN non-PNRR residual financing  
**Units:** EUR million, current prices

## 1. Executive result

The latest MUR Si.Ge.Co. v5.0 (4 September 2026) identifies a revised M4C2 portfolio of approximately **EUR 8,284.9m** across the seven research-oriented MUR investments. The portfolio comprises M4C2 I1.1 (EUR 1,800m), I1.2 (EUR 210m), I1.3 (EUR 1,610m), I1.4 (EUR 1,600m), I1.5 (EUR 1,242.8m), I3.1 (EUR 1,481.7m) and I3.3 (EUR 340.4m). The updated I3.1 and I3.3 figures differ from the older ANVUR 2026 allocation table.

The ANVUR 2026 report provides the latest measure-level cumulative expenditure snapshot identified for this exercise: approximately **EUR 5,459.8m** spent/declared across M4C2. Most measures refer to October 2025; I3.1 infrastructure spending is updated to February 2026. These are cumulative execution figures, not annual R&D expenditure.

Applying measure-specific R&D-relevance and additionality assumptions gives the following preliminary results:

| Concept | Low | Central | High |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| M4C2 PNRR expenditure reported (execution base) | 5,459.8 | 5,459.8 | 5,459.8 |
| Estimated R&D performed, including identified PRIN residual co-financing | 3,156.2 | 3,763.7 | 4,354.7 |
| Estimated additional R&D performed | **575.4** | **1,374.5** | **2,243.4** |

The PRIN DD104/2022 non-PNRR residual financing is EUR 191.8m by arithmetic (EUR 741.8m total award less EUR 550m PNRR contribution). It is shown separately and included in the combined result only as a provisional co-financing assumption: 90% execution centrally and 30% additionality. Its exact funding-source composition and realised expenditure require confirmation. The **PNRR-only central additional-R&D estimate is EUR 1,322.7m**; the combined PNRR-plus-provisional-PRIN-residual central estimate is EUR 1,374.5m.

These are model outputs, not official reported R&D values and not causal estimates. The low/central/high range is driven by explicit judgemental parameters, not statistical confidence intervals.

## 2. Perimeter

### Included in the M4C2 core

1. **M4C2 I1.1 — PNR and PRIN:** separated into EUR 970m PRIN (EUR 550m projects in being and EUR 420m new projects), EUR 230m PNR and EUR 600m researcher recruitment. Do not treat the full EUR 1.8bn as PRIN.
2. **M4C2 I1.2 — Young researchers:** research projects and contracts for ERC/MSCA/Seal of Excellence and other young researchers.
3. **M4C2 I1.3 — Extended partnerships:** 14 large programmes; official description expressly refers to basic research.
4. **M4C2 I1.4 — National Research Centres:** five centres in key enabling technologies.
5. **M4C2 I1.5 — Innovation ecosystems:** 11 ecosystems; mixed research, infrastructure, training, start-up support and local engagement.
6. **M4C2 I3.1 — Research and innovation infrastructures:** latest Si.Ge.Co. separates research infrastructures (IR) from technological innovation infrastructures (ITEC).
7. **M4C2 I3.3 — Innovative PhDs with firms:** scholarships and research in collaboration with enterprises.

### Excluded from the M4C2 headline, but retained as adjacent research measures

- **M4C1 I4.1 — Research doctorates for PA and cultural heritage:** separate extension. The MUR/ANVUR material distinguishes research doctorates from PA and cultural-heritage doctorates, but the allocation by subcategory is not yet available.
- **M4C1 I3.4 — Digital/green doctoral programmes and advanced university skills:** separate extension because the investment also includes Digital Education Hubs, transnational education and other educational activities.
- **M4C2 I2.1 IPCEI, technology-transfer centres and other MIMIT industrial innovation measures:** not part of this direct MUR research-programme aggregate. They remain in the separate industrial R&D workstream and must not be added without project-level Frascati evidence.
- **DTF/GTF venture capital:** excluded; these are financial instruments and require portfolio-company R&D data.

Total: EUR 5,459.8m. Do not interpret this as final expenditure or R&D expenditure. The execution values for I1.1 are allocated pro rata across its official subcomponents because no subcomponent-level spend split was available in the ANVUR table.

### I1.1 reconciliation

The MUR Si.Ge.Co. table decomposes the EUR 1.8bn I1.1 allocation into:
- PRIN: EUR 970m, including EUR 550m projects in being;
- PNR: EUR 230m;
- researcher recruitment fund: EUR 600m.

The EUR 550m projects-in-being cohort relates to PRIN 2020/2022 and is not automatically additional. The EUR 420m new PRIN window is treated separately. The EUR 600m recruitment fund is kept distinct from project grants to avoid double counting personnel costs.

### PRIN national residual

The MUR Seventh Report records EUR 741.8m for the DD104/2022 first PRIN window, including EUR 550m from the PNRR. The EUR 191.8m residual is calculated, not directly labelled in the cited table as a national co-financing line. It is retained as a separate provisional record and is not mixed into the PNRR execution total.

## 4. Estimation method

For each measure:

`R&D performed = reported or modelled expenditure × R&D relevance share`

`Additional R&D = R&D performed × additionality share`

The model does not multiply allocations by a blanket coefficient. Execution, R&D relevance and additionality are separate dimensions.

### Central assumptions

| Measure | R&D relevance | Additionality | Rationale |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| PRIN in-being | 100% | 10% | Eligible research activity, but much of the cohort predates/exists independently of the PNRR |
| New PRIN | 100% | 75% | New competitive projects; some deadweight/substitution remains |
| PNR | 100% | 30% | Research programme, but counterfactual and overlap with pre-existing national funding are uncertain |
| Researcher recruitment | 85% | 15% | Not all recruited researcher time is necessarily R&D; recruitment plan partly predates PNRR |
| Young researchers | 90% | 70% | Direct research projects, with some counterfactual probability of research being performed elsewhere/under other funding |
| Extended partnerships | 75% | 50% | Strong basic-research content, but mixed coordination, transfer and other costs |
| National centres | 60% | 40% | Research-heavy centres, but also infrastructure, start-up and support activity |
| Innovation ecosystems | 35% | 30% | Mixed R&D, infrastructure, training, start-up and community activity |
| Research infrastructures | 55% | 35% | Derived from the IR/ITEC mix; only the R&D-use portion of capital/infrastructure costs qualifies |
| Innovative PhDs | 50% | 40% | Only the research component of doctoral activity qualifies; training and firm co-financing need separate treatment |
| PRIN DD104 non-PNRR residual | 100% | 30% | Provisional co-financing residual; execution and additionality are assumptions |

Low/high parameters are stored in `data/processed/m4c2_rd_measure_estimate_v0_1.csv`.

## 5. Annual performance profile

The ANVUR source provides cumulative spending snapshots, not annual R&D performance. The annual profile in `data/processed/m4c2_rd_annual_shock_v0_1.csv` is therefore **modelled**, using measure-specific timing weights aligned with the start of calls, awards and implementation:
- researcher recruitment begins earlier;
- PRIN in-being and PNR are front-loaded relative to new calls;
- partnerships, national centres and ecosystems are spread across 2023–2026;
- infrastructures and doctoral programmes have larger later-year shares.

Central additional R&D profile (current prices):

| Year | Additional R&D, EUR m |
|---|---:|
| 2021 | 10.2 |
| 2022 | 36.8 |
| 2023 | 170.5 |
| 2024 | 422.3 |
| 2025 | 498.7 |
| 2026 | 236.1 |
| **Total** | **1,374.5** |

This is a provisional allocation of the cumulative model output to performance years, not observed annual expenditure. In particular, 2026 remains an estimate pending final beneficiary rendiconti and the closure data.

## 6. Other economic variables

### Cost categories

For comparability with the PRIN pilot, the central cost structure applies the Istat 2022 higher-education R&D benchmark:
- personnel: 73.12%;
- intermediate consumption: 20.27%;
- capital: 6.61%.

Applied to EUR 1,374.5m central additional R&D, this gives approximately:
- personnel: EUR 1,005.0m;
- intermediate consumption: EUR 278.6m;
- capital: EUR 90.9m.

This is a sectoral proxy, not a measured cost breakdown for M4C2. Infrastructure-heavy measures are likely to have a higher capital share.

### Research labour

Using the PRIN pilot's Istat 2023 higher-education personnel-cost proxy of EUR 56,335 per FTE-year, the central personnel component implies approximately **17,841 FTE-years**. This is a labour-input equivalent, not a count of net new jobs. It includes existing staff time and funded research labour; it should not be interpreted as employment additionality.

### Performing sectors and R&D types

No aggregate split into HERD/GOVERD/BERD or basic/applied/experimental R&D is published in the financial tables. ANVUR reports implementing-recipient categories (universities, EPR, AFAM and “other”), not the actual performing sector of every euro. In particular, the “other” category includes hubs, territorial bodies and private entities; it cannot be relabelled as BERD. The performing-sector and R&D-type splits are therefore left unquantified pending beneficiary/work-package data. Any interim split will be separately labelled as a proxy.

### Private co-financing

The MUR Si.Ge.Co. identifies private co-financing for I1.3, I1.4, I1.5, ITEC and I3.3, but the amount is not aggregated in the source table. It is excluded from the numerical estimate until actual co-financing and R&D expenditure can be traced. It must not be inferred from the PNRR allocation.

## 7. Limitations and next validation steps

1. Obtain final MUR/ReGiS measure and project rendiconti for all seven investments, with transaction dates and cost categories.
2. Reconcile I1.1 expenditure across PRIN 2020, PRIN 2022 DD104, PRIN 2022 PNRR DD1409, PNR and the researcher recruitment fund; eliminate any double counting of salaries.
3. Validate the EUR 191.8m DD104 residual funding source and actual expenditure.
4. Obtain beneficiary/work-package data for I1.3–I1.5 and I3.1 to separate research, transfer, infrastructure, training and start-up costs.
5. Split I3.1 research infrastructures (IR) from technological innovation infrastructures (ITEC) in expenditure, not just in allocation.
6. For I3.3, obtain doctoral time-use/research-cost evidence and employer co-financing.
7. Reconstruct actual annual performance profiles from transaction-level rendiconti; replace the timing weights.
8. Estimate performing-sector and R&D-type splits only after linking project beneficiaries to performing units.
9. Deflate to 2019 prices only after selecting the approved R&D deflator; this v0.1 file remains in current prices.

## 10. Critical reconciliation warnings

- **The EUR 550m PRIN amount is one aggregate, not two separate allocations.** MUR DD 1567/2022 Table 7 describes EUR 550m of PRIN projects-in-being and refers to PRIN 2020 and PRIN 2022. The MUR Seventh Report separately describes the DD104/2022 first window as EUR 741.8m total, including EUR 550m PNRR. These references must be reconciled at call/project/CUP level. This estimate counts the EUR 550m PNRR component only once; it does not add a second EUR 550m for PRIN 2020.
- **The EUR 191.8m DD104 residual is not a second PNRR allocation.** It is the arithmetic difference between the reported EUR 741.8m total first-window funding and EUR 550m PNRR contribution. Its exact source composition, execution and relationship with other national PRIN funding remain to be validated. It is included only as a separate sensitivity line.
- **Young-researcher funding is internally inconsistent across MUR reporting vintages.** The current Si.Ge.Co. and ANVUR allocation table show EUR 210m, while the MUR Seventh Report describes EUR 220m for the 2022 line and later 2025 calls. Do not add those call amounts on top of EUR 210m without a funding-source and revision crosswalk. The EUR 25.2m spending observation is the ANVUR snapshot, not a final execution total.
- **I3.1 and I3.3 allocations are vintage-sensitive.** The latest Si.Ge.Co. v5.0 amounts (EUR 1,481.7m and EUR 340.4m) are used for the allocation register; the ANVUR spending observations are retained at their stated vintage and are not overwritten.


- **I1.2 execution denominator also requires reconciliation.** ANVUR Table 1.2 reports EUR 25.2m spent against EUR 210m allocated and EUR 151.9m committed (12.0% of allocation; 16.6% of commitments). ANVUR Figure/Table 1.6 separately displays 27.7% for the “committed-spent” category. The v0.1 calculation uses the explicit EUR 25.2m amount and EUR 210m allocation, while flagging the percentage discrepancy.
