# PNRR economy and budget-law reconciliation — 2026 update

**Source vintage:** Parliamentary dossiers published September 2026.  
**Purpose:** Capture formal PNRR economies and their interaction with the 2026 Budget Law; do not treat economies as expenditure.

## 1. Budget Law 2026: resources returned from NGEU Treasury accounts

Article 1(741–743) of Law 30 December 2025, No. 199 (2026 Budget Law) provides for transfers back to State-budget revenue of resources held in the PNRR Treasury accounts that were no longer required after the November 2025 plan revision:

| Year | Amount returned (EUR bn) |
|---|---:|
| 2026 | 5.943 |
| 2027 | 1.000 |
| 2028 | 0.159 |
| **Total** | **7.102** |

The parliamentary dossier explains that the overall amount comprises approximately EUR 5.5bn of Treasury balances no longer required following the replacement of PNRR measures with nationally financed measures already provided for under existing legislation, and approximately EUR 1.6bn of economies generated by PNRR projects implemented by central administrations.

The revision preserved the Italian plan's headline EUR 194.4bn envelope, but replaced approximately EUR 14.2bn of measures: around EUR 7.8bn with new financial facilities (which may spread expenditure beyond 2026) and around EUR 6.5bn with expenditure already financed under national legislation and largely incurred in previous years. The latter must not be counted as new PNRR-induced expenditure or as additional public expenditure in the year of relabelling.

Source: [Camera dossier on DL 19/2026, articles 16–32](https://documenti.camera.it/leg19/dossier/testi/D26019avol2.htm), especially lines discussing the November 2025 revision and article 30; [Camera dossier CP0926R, September 2026](https://documenti.camera.it/leg19/dossier/testi/CP0926R.htm).

The law provides that RGS should identify the specific measures using ReGiS, after consulting the PNRR Mission Structure and the administrations concerned. ## 3. Additional project-level economies identified by September 2026

Article 7(1) of DL 162/2026 identifies a further EUR 1,698m of economies from completed PNRR projects, based on ReGiS data at 10 September 2026. Unlike the February 2026 annex, this table identifies individual measures and reports both their ReGiS budget and the economy.

The following entries are directly relevant to the R&D inventory:

| Measure | ReGiS budget (EUR m) | Economy (EUR m) | Economy / budget |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| M2C2 I3.5.1 — Hydrogen R&D, loans | 160.0 | 3.6 | 2.25% |
| M2C2 I3.5.2 — Hydrogen R&D, grants | 140.0 | 18.3 | 13.07% |
| M4C2 I1.1 — PRIN | 1,800.0 | 0.0 | 0.00% |
| M4C2 I1.2 — Young researchers | 210.0 | 55.1 | 26.24% |
| M4C2 I1.3 — Extended partnerships | 1,610.0 | 2.4 | 0.15% |
| M4C2 I1.4 — National R&D centres | 1,600.0 | 0.0 | 0.00% |
| M4C2 I1.5 — Innovation ecosystems | 1,242.8 | 4.6 | 0.37% |
| M4C2 I3.1 — Research and innovation infrastructures | 1,578.1 | 118.1 | 7.48% |

The six M4C2 measures listed above have combined ReGiS budgets of EUR 8,040.9m and reported economies of EUR 180.2m. This is only the subset listed in the parliamentary annex; it is not the full M4C2 portfolio, and it excludes M4C2 I3.3. The two hydrogen R&D measures have combined budgets of EUR 300m and economies of EUR 21.9m.

**Interpretation cautions:**
- An economy is a budget underspend / unneeded balance returned or retained in Treasury; it is not a negative R&D expenditure observation for the year in which it is returned.
- A zero economy does not prove that the full measure budget was spent, nor that the entire budget qualifies as GERD.
- M4C2 I3.1 is mixed research/innovation infrastructure; its full budget is not GERD.
- The EUR 1,698m cohort is expressly described by the dossier as additional to the EUR 1,587.510m identified earlier. Keep both cohorts separately tagged and do not add them to the EUR 7.102bn 2026–2028 Budget Law return without reconciling the legal and accounting coverage.
- The February 2026 EUR 1,587.510m annex identifies ministries, not measures. Do not assign its MUR or MIMIT totals to particular programmes by inference.

Source: [Camera dossier on DL 162/2026](https://documenti.camera.it/leg19/dossier/testi/D26162.htm), Article 7, especially the table based on ReGiS at 10 September 2026.

## 4. Data availability constraint: OpenBDAP maintenance

On 15 November 2026, the OpenBDAP open-data catalogue returned a notice that it was unavailable due to extraordinary maintenance. The catalogue's FAQ confirms that analytical datasets are normally downloadable through the catalogue and the "Analizza" section. Until service is restored, do not claim that chapter-level files have been extracted or harmonised.

The project retains the dataset register and should resume extraction when the catalogue is available. In the meantime, use parliamentary dossiers, the official Budget Law annexes and published final accounts as independent cross-checks.

## 5. Implications for the R&D shock reconstruction

The new evidence supports four distinct treatments:

1. **National expenditure already in the baseline:** approximately EUR 6.5bn of measures re-labelled as PNRR in the November 2025 revision must not be counted as new spending when the PNRR label is applied.
2. **Unused PNRR resources:** the EUR 7.102bn return is a financing/budget operation, not new negative spending in 2026–2028.
3. **Programme-specific R&D economies:** use the EUR 21.9m hydrogen and EUR 180.2m MUR programme economies as reconciliation controls, not as direct GERD adjustments.
4. **Unallocated ministry-level economies:** keep the EUR 208.879m MIMIT and EUR 120.749m MUR February 2026 amounts in a separate, unallocated bucket pending the RGS/ReGiS measure-level breakdown.

This strengthens the requirement to construct an annual funding/expenditure bridge before estimating additional R&D performed in Italy.
