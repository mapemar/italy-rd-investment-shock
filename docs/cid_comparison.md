# Italy PNRR CID version history and R&D-relevant measure crosswalk

**Status: first evidence-backed extraction; not yet a complete measure-by-measure legal diff.**  
**Cut-off: 15 November 2026.**

## 1. Executive findings

The official Commission country page lists the original 2021 plan and successive revisions in September 2023, December 2023, April 2024, November 2024, June 2025, November 2025, March 2026 and August 2026. The latest adopted version is the August 2026 Council Implementing Decision, Council document **ST 12357/26**, with annex **ST 12357/26 ADD 1**. Its recitals expressly list all earlier Council amendments: 19 September 2023, 8 December 2023, 14 May 2024, 18 November 2024, 20 June 2025, 27 November 2025 and 30 March 2026. The August 2026 amendment was requested on 11 June 2026 and concerns 118 measures. The official Commission page and CID are the master version register.

The 2026 annex is a replacement annex, not a simple list of deltas. A robust crosswalk must compare stable investment identities and milestone/target codes across each full annex. Codes can change or be supplemented by bis/ter suffixes; code matching alone is insufficient.

## 4. What is verified in the August 2026 CID and annex

The August 2026 CID recitals establish that:
- the plan had already been amended on seven occasions before August 2026;
- Italy submitted its latest reasoned request on 11 June 2026;
- the latest amendment concerns 118 measures;
- examples of amended candidate measures include M4C2-9 (national R&D leaders), M4C2-14 (technology transfer centres), M4C2-16bis (research and innovation infrastructures), M4C2-18bis (innovation ecosystems), M4C2-21 / M4C2-21bis (Digital Transition Fund), M2C2-43 / M2C2-42bis (Green Transition Fund), and M1C2-34 (Transition 4.0 scale-up).

Specific annex evidence:
- **M4C2-7 / Investment 1.1 PRIN:** at least 900 fixed-term researchers to be hired (annex p. 319).
- **M4C2-8 / Investment 1.3 extended partnerships and basic research:** 14 research partnerships; calls include research and researcher recruitment conditions (annex pp. 319–320).
- **M4C2-9 / Investment 1.4 national R&D leaders:** activity reports for five national centres (annex pp. 319–320).
- **M4C2-14 / Investment 2.3 technology transfer centres:** disbursement target of EUR 309 million by Q2 2026 (annex pp. 322 and 458). This is not automatically EUR 309 million of R&D.
- **M4C2-16bis / Investment 3.1 research and innovation infrastructures:** activity reports for at least 30 research or innovation infrastructure projects (annex p. 324).
- **M4C2-18bis / Investment 1.5 innovation ecosystems:** activity reports for at least 10 ecosystems (annex p. 327).
- **M4C2-21bis / Investment 3.2 Digital Transition Fund:** transfer of EUR 400 million by Q4 2024 (annex p. 330). Equity injection is a financial transaction, not R&D expenditure.
- **M2C2-42bis / Investment 5.4 Green Transition Fund:** transfer of EUR 250 million by Q4 2024 (annex p. 227). Equity injection is a financial transaction, not R&D expenditure.
- **M6C2 Investment 2.1 / biomedical research:** explicitly finances Proof of Concept and research initiatives on rare diseases, rare cancers and other high-impact diseases; target of at least 200 funded projects (annex pp. 390–393; target code M6C2-3).
- **M1C2 Investment 1 / Transition 4.0:** the annex describes tax credits for tangible and intangible capital goods, R&D and innovation activities, and training (annex pp. 99–101). Aggregate Transition 4.0 credit counts or fiscal cost must not be treated as R&D expenditure.

## 6. Initial financial evidence and gaps

- The original national financial plan identifies EUR 1.8 billion for M4C2 Investment 1.1 (PNR/PRIN), including EUR 1.38 billion in “projects in being” and EUR 0.42 billion in new projects. MUR implementation documentation decomposes the in-being component into researcher hiring, PNR and PRIN funding. This is an important additionality risk: incorporation of an existing national programme into the PNRR does not establish that all associated expenditure is incremental.
- The original national financial plan lists EUR 432 million for M4C1 Investment 4.1 on research doctorates and innovative doctorates. This is a programme envelope, not a GERD estimate.
- The latest CID annex records EUR 400 million for DTF and EUR 250 million for GTF as fund-transfer milestones. These are financial transactions and must not enter the R&D shock directly.
- The latest annex records EUR 309 million for technology transfer centres as a disbursement target. The R&D-eligible portion remains to be identified.
- Measure-level initial and revised allocations for other candidates have not yet been reconciled across all CID annexes. No missing amount has been estimated or imputed.
