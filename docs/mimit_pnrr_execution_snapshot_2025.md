# MIMIT PNRR execution snapshot — 31 December 2025

**Purpose:** add the latest MIMIT implementation evidence to the R&D / extended innovation shock database.  
**Source:** MIMIT, *Relazione sullo stato di attuazione del PNRR – II semestre 2025*, dated 31 December 2025.  
**Source URL:** https://www.mimit.gov.it/images/stories/documenti/allegati/pnrr/31122025_Mimit_Report_Semestrale_DEF_.pdf

## 1. Venture capital funds: new operational evidence

The MIMIT report confirms the strategic scope and implementation model of both funds: direct investments in start-ups and SMEs with innovative, scalable projects, and indirect investments through third-party VC funds.

| Indicator (31 Dec 2025) | GTF – M2C2 I5.4 | DTF – M4C2 I3.2 |
|---|---:|---:|
| PNRR envelope | EUR 250m | EUR 400m |
| Resources committed (direct, indirect and costs) | approx. 70% | approx. 70% |
| Direct start-ups | 8 | 16 |
| Third-party VC funds | 3 | 6 |
| Start-ups invested by underlying funds | 6 | 28 |
| Total investee companies | 14 | 44 |
| Additional jobs vs investment date | 95 | 132 |

Combined: 58 investee companies and 227 additional jobs (sum of the two fund-level MIMIT figures).

Applying the 70% commitment ratio to each fund's envelope gives an indicative EUR 175m GTF and EUR 280m DTF, or EUR 455m combined. This is a derived commitment indicator, not a cash-deployment figure. The report explicitly says the 70% covers direct and indirect investments **and costs**. It must therefore not be labelled as EUR 455m invested in companies or as EUR 455m of R&D.

### Reconciliation with CDP's 74% deliberations indicator

CDP's 2025 annual report reports cumulative investment deliberations equal to 74% of total investible resources, while MIMIT reports approximately 70% of each fund's total resources committed, including costs. These are different metrics with different denominators / accounting scope. Do not force them to reconcile or add them together.

For the user's adopted modelling convention, the funds remain included at a 100% coefficient in the **extended innovation shock**. The best available fund-level implementation indicator at 31 December 2025 is EUR 455m of indicative commitments (including costs); the CDP 74% deliberations metric remains a separate approval/pipeline indicator. Neither is a measure of annual cash deployment or official GERD/BERD.

## 2. Other R&D-relevant MIMIT measures

The same MIMIT report provides a financial progress snapshot for selected measures:

| Measure | Envelope | Reported financial progress | Progress ratio | Treatment |

| IPCEI (M4C2 I2.1) | EUR 1,500m | EUR 215.36m | 14.67% | Mixed R&D, innovation and first industrial deployment |
| Innovation Agreements (M4C2 I2.2bis) | EUR 164m | EUR 11.38m | 6.94% | R&D-relevant instrument; actual project cost composition still required |
| Technology Transfer Centres (M4C2 I2.3) | EUR 350m | EUR 46.95m | 13.41% | Mixed: centre operations/services and company R&D |
| Net Zero and strategic supply chains (M2C2 I5.1) | EUR 3,500m | EUR 698.87m | 19.97% | Mixed productive investment, decarbonisation and innovation |

These values are taken from the report's measure-level financial table. They are not automatically R&D expenditure. In particular, the IPCEI instrument covers R&D, innovation and first industrial production; Net Zero is predominantly a wider industrial investment measure.

For Technology Transfer Centres, the report also states that more than 6,500 firms had been reached and more than 420 R&D projects financed. This is a useful output indicator, but the value of R&D expenditure in those projects is not given in the same passage.

## 3. Implications for the shock estimate

1. The DTF/GTF component can now be reported with portfolio reach and employment outcomes, not only fund envelopes.
2. Keep the 100% user-approved coefficient as an **extended innovation proxy**, but phase it according to observed deployment when transaction-level schedules are obtained. Until then, use the EUR 455m commitment indicator as a cumulative implementation proxy, not as annual expenditure.
3. For IPCEI, Innovation Agreements and Technology Transfer Centres, the reported financial progress is an upper-level implementation indicator. It must be screened for the share of R&D actually performed in Italy and the timing of that activity.
4. Do not allocate the whole Net Zero measure to R&D. Require project-level R&D work-package evidence.
5. The report provides a status as at 31 December 2025, not a full annual cash or accrual series for 2020–2025. No annual flow should be inferred from the cumulative snapshots.
