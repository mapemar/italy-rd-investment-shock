# First official implementation snapshots: MUR and MIMIT

**Status:** first source-based quantitative extraction. These are cumulative snapshots as at 30 November 2025, not annual expenditure series and not R&D expenditure.

## 1. Administrative totals

| Administration | PNRR allocation (€m) | Of which financial instruments (€m) | Reported PNRR spending (€m) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| MUR | 11,583.01 | 599.00 | 7,343.19 |
| MIMIT | 29,977.00 | 7,355.00 | 20,600.44 |

The Seventh PNRR Report also provides a net-of-financial-instruments view:
- MUR: allocation €10,984.01m; spending €7,343.19m.
- MIMIT: allocation €22,622.00m; spending €19,903.70m.

These totals are administration-level and cannot be assigned to individual R&D measures.

The report classifies these as financial instruments or assimilated measures. Their reported "spending" must not be read as final beneficiaries' R&D expenditure or as macroeconomic impact.

## 3. MUR implementation outputs available from the same report

- PRIN: 5,533 projects admitted to funding against the 5,350 milestone; 916 of 3,280 contracted type-B researchers were directly involved in PNRR projects associated with PRIN, extended partnerships, National Centres and innovation ecosystems.
- Extended partnerships: 14 partnerships selected and funded; 1,698 fixed-term researchers hired by 23 June 2025 against a 1,400 target (121%).

These are programme outputs, not annual expenditure. The 916 researcher count is aggregated across four MUR investment lines and must not be allocated to PRIN alone.

## 4. Consequence for annual shock estimation

The public report supports:
1. a cumulative MUR/MIMIT implementation snapshot at 30 November 2025;
2. selected instrument-level cumulative figures for DTF, GTF, IPCEI and industrial funds;
3. output indicators for PRIN and extended partnerships.

It does **not** support a measure-by-year expenditure allocation for 2020–2025. Annual cells therefore remain blank pending measure-level ReGiS extracts, implementing-body accounts, beneficiary reports or annual financial statements.
