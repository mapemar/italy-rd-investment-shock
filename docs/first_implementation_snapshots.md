**Status:** first source-based quantitative extraction. These are cumulative snapshots, not annual expenditure series and not R&D expenditure.

## 3. PRIN cumulative expenditure progress at 31 December 2024

The Court of Auditors' May 2025 report, based on ReGiS information available in February 2025, states that at 31 December 2024 the PRIN/PNR line had a spending progress rate of 56.3% against an allocation of €1.8bn. The implied cumulative amount is approximately €1,013.4m (€1,800m × 56.3%). This is derived from a rounded percentage, not an exact reported euro amount. It is a cumulative end-2024 snapshot, not expenditure incurred during 2024 alone.

## 4. DTF and GTF fund-manager transfer milestones

The same Court of Auditors report documents the transfer milestones to CDP Venture Capital SGR:
- DTF: €150m had already been transferred under the previous framework; a further €250m was subsequently transferred, completing the €400m transfer milestone. The additional €100m subscription mentioned in the sequence is part of the revised funding path and must not be added again to the €400m cumulative total.
- GTF: €125m had already been transferred and the remaining €125m was subsequently transferred, completing the €250m transfer milestone.

These transfers are fund-level financial transactions. They are not startup R&D, startup investment deployment or additional GDP. A reconciliation is required because the November 2025 snapshot reports €66.05m for DTF and €54.34m for GTF in its financial-instrument spending table. These values are not directly comparable to the fund-manager transfer milestones until definitions, dates and accounting layers are reconciled.

## 5. MUR implementation outputs

- PRIN: 5,533 projects admitted to funding against the 5,350 milestone; 916 of 3,280 contracted type-B researchers were directly involved in PNRR projects associated with PRIN, extended partnerships, National Centres and innovation ecosystems.
- Extended partnerships: 14 partnerships selected and funded; 1,698 fixed-term researchers hired by 23 June 2025 against a 1,400 target (121%).

These are programme outputs, not annual expenditure. The 916 researcher count is aggregated across four MUR investment lines and must not be allocated to PRIN alone.

## 6. Consequence for annual shock estimation

The public reports support cumulative implementation snapshots and selected programme outputs. They do **not** support a full measure-by-year expenditure allocation for 2020–2025. Annual cells therefore remain blank pending measure-level ReGiS extracts, implementing-body accounts, beneficiary reports or annual financial statements.
