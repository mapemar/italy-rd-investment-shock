# MIMIT annual shock estimation — evidence update (16 November 2026)

## 1. New official evidence

The MIMIT second-semester 2025 PNRR monitoring report (data mainly at 31 December 2025) states:
- Total MIMIT PNRR declared expenditure in ReGiS: EUR 21.243bn, 70.8% of its EUR 29.977bn allocation. This is an aggregate for all MIMIT measures and must not be treated as R&D.
- The report explicitly distinguishes financial facilities from ordinary grants. For DTF and GTF, implementation is considered completed when the full resources are transferred/legally committed to the implementing partner and CDP Venture Capital then invests directly or indirectly in startups, incubators/accelerators or VC funds. Thus transfer to CDP is not equivalent to final startup investment or R&D performance.
- The financial-circuit table records two requests for disbursement for EUR 400m for DTF and two for EUR 250m for GTF. These are facility-level transfers/requests, not annual investment in portfolio companies.
- For technology transfer centres (M4C2 I2.3), the Court of Auditors' 2025 report records approximately EUR 75m of expenditure reported by the centres, around 22% of the total allocation. This is the closest measure-specific actual-spending figure currently identified in the reviewed sources. It includes technology-transfer-centre activities and must not automatically be classified entirely as Frascati R&D.
- IPCEI (EUR 1.5bn), innovation agreements (EUR 164m), Net Zero (EUR 3.5bn after revision), and strategic supply chains (EUR 750m) are allocations or project-selection values in the reviewed monitoring tables; they are not annual R&D expenditure.

## 2. Why we cannot derive a 2020–2025 annual MIMIT R&D shock from the headline expenditure figure

The MIMIT report's EUR 21.243bn cumulative declared expenditure covers a very broad portfolio, including Transizione 4.0, Net Zero, supply chains, energy self-production and other measures. Its own discussion says the expenditure profile is mainly driven by Transizione 4.0 in 2021–2023. This aggregate cannot be apportioned to R&D without measure-level and cost-category data.

The report also provides a chart of planned, committed and declared spending by year, but does not publish a machine-readable annual table with measure-level values. Digitising the chart would at best produce approximate aggregate annual MIMIT expenditure, not the R&D shock. It is therefore not used as a proxy for R&D.

## 3. Updated annual-shock treatment

| Instrument | Evidence available | Treatment in the shock |
|---|---|---|
| DTF | EUR 400m facility requests by end-2025; CDP cumulative deliberations 74% of total DTF+GTF resources | Count 100% of actual fund investment deliberations in the extended innovation proxy, by year where identifiable. Do not use the EUR 400m transfer as annual startup investment. |
| GTF | EUR 250m facility requests by end-2025; EUR 15.65m disclosed GTF company investments by end-2024 | Same proxy treatment; GTF company investment is a validation/subset, not additive. |
| Technology transfer centres | Approx. EUR 75m reported expenditure, about 22% allocation | Record as mixed technology-transfer activity; classify R&D share only with eligible cost breakdown. |
| IPCEI / innovation agreements | Allocations and project registers; no robust annual eligible expenditure series yet | Build project-level R&D cost and performance-year schedule; do not apply 100% to total grants automatically. |
| Net Zero / supply chains | Large allocations, predominantly productive/industrial investment with mixed R&D content | Only explicitly eligible R&D work packages enter official R&D; broader technology investment may be reported separately. |
| Transizione 4.0 / 5.0 | Very large fiscal/investment measures; broad MIMIT expenditure is dominated by these | Do not count capital goods, energy efficiency, training or ordinary digitalisation as R&D. Include only eligible R&D tax-credit component with fiscal/accrual timing. |

## 4. Consequence for the annual series

We can now add the following evidence markers to the annual database:
- 2025: EUR 21.243bn cumulative MIMIT declared expenditure across all measures (context only, not R&D).
- by end-2025: EUR 650m facility-level DTF/GTF requests (transfer to CDP; not portfolio deployment).
- 2025 report: EUR 75m cumulative reported expenditure for technology transfer centres (mixed category; no annual breakdown or R&D share).
- 2025: CDP DTF/GTF deliberations at 74% of total investible capital. The cumulative amount and 2025 increment remain the extended VC innovation proxy, separate from official GERD.

No additional annual MIMIT R&D shock is entered yet because the underlying annual eligible-cost data are not in the report.
